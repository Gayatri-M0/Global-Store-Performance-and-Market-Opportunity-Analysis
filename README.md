# Global-Store-Performance-and-Market-Opportunity-Analysis
An SQL-based analysis of a global electronics retailer focused on evaluating store and market performance, profitability, sales efficiency, product mix and customer contribution to identify high-performing markets and potential business opportunities.

---

## 1. Dataset Resource
The dataset for Global Store Performance and Market Opportunity Analysis is downloaded from Maven Analytics website.
https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/global-electronics-retailer
