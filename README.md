# Global-Store-Performance-and-Market-Opportunity-Analysis
An SQL-based analysis of a global electronics retailer focused on evaluating store and market performance, profitability, sales efficiency, product mix and customer contribution to identify high-performing markets and potential business opportunities. The business focuses on electronics items such as TV and Videos, mobile phones, Audios, Home Appliances, Computers, Cameras, etc which are sold at global-level via multiple stores and online platform. This dataset is useful for analyzing a global operating company and function. To know whether these type of company are successfully running or need some changes in business operations.
The main goal of any small-scale and large-scale business to have minimum loss and maximum profit. All the parameters during analysis are focused by keeping this POV.

## Business Problem
- Understanding sales and profitability of stores across different markets.
- Identifying underperforming stores.
- Evaluating high and low demand of products and its categories.
- Understanding customer purchasing behaviour.
- Identifying business growth opportunities.

## Project Objective
- Evaluate overall business performance.
- Identify revenue and profit trends.
- Analyse geographical sales performance.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming products and stores.
- Examine customer purchasing patterns.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.
